# Check Play Store Rollout Status

## Why You Might Not Have the Update Yet

Play Store releases can be **gradual** (staged rollout):
- 10% rollout → Only 10% of users get the update
- 50% rollout → Half of users get it
- 100% rollout → Everyone gets it immediately

---

## How to Check Rollout Percentage

1. Go to: https://play.google.com/console
2. Click on **PulseMate Connect**
3. Go to **Production** track
4. Look at the **current release**
5. Check if it shows a percentage (e.g., "10% rollout")

---

## If It's a Staged Rollout

You have 3 options:

### Option 1: Increase Rollout to 100%
1. In Play Console → Production
2. Click **"Manage rollout"**
3. Select **"Complete rollout"** or increase to 100%
4. Wait 10-15 minutes
5. Check for updates on your device

### Option 2: Join as Tester
1. In Play Console → Testing → Internal testing
2. Add your Google account email
3. Use the testing link on your device
4. You'll get updates faster

### Option 3: Wait
- Staged rollouts can take 1-7 days to reach all users
- Check back in a few hours
