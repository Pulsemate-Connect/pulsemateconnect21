═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  OPTION 1: USE YOUR PHYSICAL PHONE (RECOMMENDED) ⭐
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This is the FASTEST and EASIEST way!

STEP 1: Enable USB Debugging on your phone
  1. Open Settings → About Phone
  2. Tap "Build Number" 7 times (enables Developer Mode)
  3. Go back → Developer Options
  4. Enable "USB Debugging"
  5. Confirm the popup

STEP 2: Connect phone via USB cable to computer

STEP 3: Check connection:
  Open PowerShell and run:
  > adb devices
  
  You should see your device listed like:
  List of devices attached
  ABC123XYZ    device

STEP 4: Build and install:
  > cd "c:\Users\shubh\Desktop\PulseMate Connect\pulsemateconnect21"
  > npx expo run:android --device

  ⏱️ Wait 3-5 minutes for build to complete
  📱 App will auto-install on your phone

STEP 5: Test!
  - Open PulseMate Connect on your phone
  - Go to Profile tab
  - Click "Edit Profile"
  - See ProfileWizard with 6 steps ✅

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: "adb devices" shows nothing

Solution:
  1. Check USB cable is data cable (not charge-only)
  2. On phone: Revoke USB debugging authorizations, then re-enable
  3. Try different USB port
  4. Install phone's USB drivers (Google for your phone model)

Problem: Emulator quit before opening

Solution:
  1. Start emulator manually first (see Option 2)
  2. Or use physical phone instead (Option 1)

Problem: Build fails with errors

Solution:
  1. Clean build:
     Remove-Item -Recurse -Force android\app\build
     Remove-Item -Recurse -Force android\.gradle
  2. Try again
