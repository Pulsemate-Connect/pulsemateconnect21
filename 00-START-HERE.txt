💡 KEY INSIGHT - WHY THIS WORKS:

   Firebase has TWO modes for phone authentication:

   Development/Web (expo-firebase-recaptcha):
   - Shows reCAPTCHA modal to user
   - Prevents SMS abuse during development
   - Requires expo-firebase-core (BROKEN)
   - Used in Expo Go and browsers

   Production Android (SafetyNet attestation):
   - No visible reCAPTCHA modal
   - Automatic device verification by Google
   - Works when SHA-256 registered (YOU ALREADY DID THIS!)
   - More secure than reCAPTCHA
   - NO expo-firebase-recaptcha needed!

   The build was failing because we were importing expo-firebase-recaptcha
   (which requires expo-firebase-core). But in production builds, we don't
   need it at all! Firebase automatically uses SafetyNet instead.
