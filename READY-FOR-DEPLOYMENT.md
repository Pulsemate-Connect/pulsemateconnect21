## 📊 What Changed

### From Firebase → 2Factor

**Before (Firebase):**
- ❌ Unreliable OTP delivery
- ❌ Complex setup (reCAPTCHA, Play Integrity)
- ❌ Doesn't work on all devices
- ❌ Hit free tier limits

**After (2Factor):**
- ✅ Reliable SMS delivery (5-10 seconds)
- ✅ Simple API
- ✅ Works everywhere
- ✅ Cost-effective (₹0.15/SMS)
- ✅ Indian provider (fast in India)
