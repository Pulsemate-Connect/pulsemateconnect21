# 🚀 Force Update to Version 71 - Quick Guide

**Problem:** Still seeing "Configuration error" after uploading Version 71  
**Reason:** Your device hasn't received the update yet

---

## ✅ **SOLUTION 1: Uninstall & Reinstall (Fastest)**

### Steps:
1. **Long-press** PulseMate Connect app icon
2. Click **"Uninstall"**
3. Go to **Play Store**
4. Search **"PulseMate Connect"**
5. Click **"Install"**
6. Wait for installation
7. Open app and test OTP login

**Expected Result:** ✅ OTP should work now!

---

## ✅ **SOLUTION 2: Force Play Store Update**

### Steps:
1. Open **Play Store** app
2. Tap your **profile icon** (top right)
3. Tap **"Manage apps & device"**
4. Under **"Updates available"**, look for PulseMate Connect
5. If you see it, tap **"Update"**
6. If not, tap **"Check for updates"** at the top
7. Wait a few minutes and check again

---

## ✅ **SOLUTION 3: Join Internal Testing Track**

This gives you **instant access** to new versions:

### Steps:
1. Go to Play Console: https://play.google.com/console
2. Click **PulseMate Connect**
3. Left menu → **Testing** → **Internal testing**
4. Click **"Testers"** tab
5. Add your Google account email
6. Save
7. Copy the **"Testing link"**
8. Open that link on your device
9. Click **"Become a tester"**
10. Download the app from that link

**Expected Result:** ✅ You'll always get updates immediately!

---

## ✅ **SOLUTION 4: Check Rollout Percentage**

### Steps:
1. Go to: https://play.google.com/console
2. Click **PulseMate Connect**
3. Go to **Production** track
4. Check if it shows percentage (e.g., "10% rollout")
5. If yes, click **"Manage rollout"**
6. Click **"Complete rollout"** to make it 100%
7. Wait 10-15 minutes
8. Check for updates on device

---

## 🔍 **How to Verify You Have Version 71**

After updating, check the version:

### In the App:
1. Open app
2. Go to **Settings** or **Profile**
3. Look for **"About"** or **"Version"**
4. Should show: **Version 1.3.4 (71)**

### In Play Store:
1. Open **Play Store**
2. Search **"PulseMate Connect"**
3. Check **"Updated on"** date
4. Should show: **Aug 01, 2026**
