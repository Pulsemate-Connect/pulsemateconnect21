║         📱 HOW TO CONNECT YOUR ANDROID DEVICE VIA USB            ║
║                                                                   ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

⚠️  YOUR DEVICE IS NOT DETECTED YET

Follow these steps to connect your Android phone:

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 1️⃣  ENABLE DEVELOPER OPTIONS (First time only)

   On your Android phone:
   
   1. Open Settings
   2. Scroll to "About phone" (or "About device")
   3. Find "Build number"
   4. Tap "Build number" 7 times rapidly
   5. You'll see: "You are now a developer!"

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 2️⃣  ENABLE USB DEBUGGING

   On your Android phone:
   
   1. Go back to Settings
   2. Look for "Developer options" (might be under "System")
   3. Scroll down and find "USB debugging"
   4. Toggle it ON (turn it green)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 3️⃣  CONNECT USB CABLE

   1. Get a good USB cable (data cable, not charging-only)
   2. Connect phone to your PC
   3. Your phone will vibrate or make a sound

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 4️⃣  ALLOW USB DEBUGGING ON YOUR PHONE

   A popup will appear on your phone:
   
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Allow USB debugging?                │
   │                                     │
   │ The computer's RSA key fingerprint │
   │ is: XX:XX:XX...                     │
   │                                     │
   │ ☑ Always allow from this computer  │ ← CHECK THIS!
   │                                     │
   │         [CANCEL]    [OK]            │ ← TAP OK!
   └─────────────────────────────────────┘

   IMPORTANT: Check the "Always allow" box!

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 5️⃣  CHANGE USB MODE (If needed)

   If your phone is in "Charging only" mode:
   
   1. Pull down notification shade on phone
   2. You'll see "Charging this device via USB"
   3. Tap it
   4. Select "File Transfer" or "PTP" or "MTP"

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 6️⃣  VERIFY CONNECTION

   Run this batch file:
   
   check-device.bat
   
   Or run this command:
   
   adb devices
   
   You should see:
   
   List of devices attached
   RF8M12345AB     device
   ↑
   Your device ID

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 7️⃣  RUN THE APP

   Once your device shows, run:
   
   run-on-device.bat
   
   Or:
   
   npx expo run:android
   
   This will:
   ✅ Build the app (5-10 minutes first time)
   ✅ Install on your phone
   ✅ Launch automatically

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING

   ❌ Device not showing?
      
      Try these:
      
      1. Different USB cable (must be data cable)
      2. Different USB port on your PC
      3. Restart ADB:
         adb kill-server
         adb start-server
         adb devices
      
      4. Restart your phone
      5. Disable and re-enable USB debugging
      6. Check if drivers are installed (Windows)

   ❌ Device shows "unauthorized"?
      
      1. Disconnect USB cable
      2. Revoke USB debugging authorizations:
         Settings → Developer options → Revoke USB debugging
      3. Reconnect USB cable
      4. Allow USB debugging again (check "Always allow")

   ❌ Device shows "offline"?
      
      1. Restart your phone
      2. Run: adb kill-server && adb start-server
